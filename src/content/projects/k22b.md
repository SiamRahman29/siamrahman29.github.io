## What it is

K22B is a fast-shipping web-dev garage based in Dhaka, founded in 2026. The pitch is deliberately narrow: briefs go in, live sites come out. No retainers, no committees, no slide decks about slide decks — you talk straight to the person building your site.

This project is the garage's own site, and it doubles as its calling card: if the shop claims to ship web fast, its own front door had better prove it.

## How it's built

Astro for a content-driven, mostly-static site that ships almost no JavaScript by default — the right tool when the goal is a fast, cheap-to-host marketing site. Tailwind CSS for styling, TypeScript across the board, and GitHub Pages for zero-cost hosting on a custom base path.

## Why it exists

Small businesses and individuals who need a real website keep getting quoted agency timelines and agency prices for what is, honestly, a few days of focused work. K22B is a bet that there's demand for the opposite: direct, fast, and no ceremony. The site itself is the proof of concept — built and deployed with the same speed the shop sells.
